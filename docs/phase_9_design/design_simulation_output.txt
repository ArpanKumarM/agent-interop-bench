SUMMARY
  best design by worst-DGP P(correct+confident) at th in (0.45, 0.583, 0.85): 80x2  (1280 N+P trials).
  RECOMMENDED allocation: 64 x 3  (8 domains x 8 scenarios x 3 repeats
  = 1536 N+P trials). More scenarios-per-domain -- not more repeats --
  shrinks the WITHIN-domain scenario-variance term that drives the
  fixed-domain interval, so 64x3 gives markedly better Q1 power than
  40x5 at ~the same cost. 64x4 (2048) adds little; 80x2 (1280) is the
  budget floor but R=2 hurts Q2 and attrition-robustness.
